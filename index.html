Product demos, compiled.

A starter scene for your own video, plus the scenes behind the published OutcomeOps videos. Each one is a small React app on a single animation clock, timed to narration timestamps and rendered in headless Chromium. No video editor was opened for any of them. Click Preview to play the live scene in your browser, or Source to read how it was built.

This repo on GitHub YouTube channel OutcomeOps (Apache 2.0) How it works

Previews play silently. Narration audio is not committed, so scenes run without sound until you synthesize it with your own ElevenLabs key. Serve the repo root over HTTP, not file://: python3 -m http.server 8765, then open http://localhost:8765/. The finished, narrated videos are on the YouTube channel.
